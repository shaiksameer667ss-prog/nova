# NOVA Intelligence Vision

Status: Research north-star
Programme: NOVA Intelligence Lab
Ambition: Progress towards increasingly general artificial intelligence, with artificial superintelligence as a long-term research aspiration.

## 1. Mission

Develop NOVA into an increasingly general, capable, adaptive and reliable AI system.

NOVA should progressively improve its ability to reason, learn, plan, understand unfamiliar environments, transfer knowledge, use tools, conduct research and discover effective solutions to difficult problems.

Artificial superintelligence is an ambition, not an established capability or guaranteed outcome.

## 2. What intelligence means in this programme

NOVA must demonstrate measurable improvement in:

1. Reasoning: solving difficult and unfamiliar problems.
2. Generalization: applying knowledge beyond training and development examples.
3. Continual learning: retaining useful knowledge while adapting to new information.
4. Transfer: applying learned methods across different tasks and domains.
5. Planning: achieving long-horizon goals under constraints and uncertainty.
6. Causal understanding: predicting consequences and identifying underlying mechanisms.
7. Memory: retaining and retrieving relevant experiences reliably.
8. Scientific discovery: generating hypotheses and testing them against evidence.
9. Adaptation: selecting appropriate strategies for changing environments.
10. Self-improvement: developing better methods and demonstrating improvements against controlled baselines.
11. Efficiency: improving capability relative to compute, time and cost.
12. Safety: remaining controllable, auditable and appropriately bounded.

These dimensions must be measured separately. A single aggregate score must not conceal capability failures.

## 3. Target architecture

### 3.1 Foundation-model interface

Support interchangeable model providers, model versions and inference configurations.

Record exact model identity, provider, configuration and usage for every experiment.

### 3.2 Intelligence controller

Coordinate reasoning, planning, memory retrieval, tools, verification and stopping decisions.

The controller must distinguish model proposals from observed environment outcomes.

### 3.3 Reasoning and search engine

Research alternative reasoning methods, decomposition, candidate generation, search, critique, verification and adaptive inference budgets.

### 3.4 Continual learning engine

Convert verified experience into reusable knowledge and skills.

Evaluate retention, adaptation, forgetting, interference and transfer.

### 3.5 Memory and knowledge system

Support working, episodic, semantic and procedural knowledge.

Preserve provenance, confidence, revision history and retrieval evidence.

### 3.6 World and causal models

Develop predictive representations of environments, dependencies, state transitions and action consequences.

### 3.7 Evolution and architecture research

Generate candidate improvements to NOVA's prompts, algorithms, controllers and cognitive architecture.

Evaluate candidates independently in isolated environments.

Never allow a candidate to approve its own success.

### 3.8 Scientific research engine

Support literature research, question formulation, hypotheses, experiment design, execution, analysis and reproducibility.

Distinguish generated hypotheses from experimentally established findings.

### 3.9 Intelligence evaluation and safety plane

Maintain independent evaluation, permissions, sandboxing, audit records, rollback and human authorization.

The evaluation system must not be controlled by the candidate being evaluated.

## 4. Fundamental research principles

- Evidence is more important than persuasive explanations.
- Held-out generalization is more important than memorization.
- Learning must produce measurable future benefit.
- More agents, longer prompts and more computation do not automatically mean more intelligence.
- Improvements must be compared under controlled resource budgets.
- Regressions and negative results must be preserved.
- Self-modification must be isolated, reversible and independently evaluated.
- Human authorization remains necessary for consequential actions.
- Security and evaluation controls must not be weakened to improve scores.
- No benchmark result alone establishes AGI or superintelligence.

## 5. Current capability boundary

NOVA currently has a model-client interface, an autonomous software-engineering controller, planning experiments, persistent SQLite memory, behavioral verification and software-engineering evaluation infrastructure.

These are research foundations, not evidence of general intelligence.

The first Intelligence Lab programme will establish a broader benchmark before claiming improvement in general reasoning or learning.

## 6. Long-term objective

Progressively develop a system that can learn, reason, adapt, research and improve its problem-solving methods across increasingly diverse domains.

Every claimed improvement must be supported by reproducible evidence.
